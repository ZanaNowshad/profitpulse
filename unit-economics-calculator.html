What is unit economics?

Unit economics measure the direct revenue and cost of a single sale. They answer the question that matters most: do we make money on each customer after we pay to acquire them? For SaaS founders, three metrics rule everything: gross margin per unit, contribution after CAC, and LTV:CAC.

Gross margin per unit

Price − Variable cost. Your gross margin rate should typically be well above 70% for software. If it isn't, your pricing model or cost structure needs work before you scale ads.

Contribution after CAC

(Price − Variable cost) − CAC. This is the single most important number in a startup. If it's positive per unit, scaling acquisition is a lever. If it's negative, growth burns money faster than it creates it.

LTV : CAC and breakeven

Lifetime value ÷ acquisition cost tells you how much each acquired customer returns. A ratio below 1.5 is a red flag; 3+ is scalable. Breakeven customers (fixed cost ÷ contribution) tells you exactly how many paying customers cover your monthly fixed costs.
