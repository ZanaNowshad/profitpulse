Gross margin vs. contribution margin

Gross margin is revenue minus the variable cost of delivering your product. It answers: does a unit cover its own production cost? For software this is very high (70\u201390%); for physical or service businesses it is lower.

Contribution margin goes one step further and subtracts the cost to acquire that unit (CAC). A business can have excellent gross margin but still be unprofitable if acquisition is expensive. ProfitPulse shows both so you never mistake one for the other.

How to read your margin

  • Gross margin rate > 70% — strong for software; scale it.
  • 50\u201370% — workable worldwide, but review cost and pricing levers.
  • < 50% — inspect ads, hosting, support, and COGS before you invest in growth.
