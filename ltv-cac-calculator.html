Target-Margin Pricing

What is LTV:CAC and why it matters

Lifetime value (LTV) is the gross profit a customer generates over their whole lifetime. Customer acquisition cost (CAC) is what you pay to win them. The ratio LTV ÷ CAC is the single most important metric in a SaaS business — it tells you whether acquiring customers is a growth lever or a money pit.

Reading the ratio

  • Below 1.5 — red flag. You're spending more to acquire than the customer is worth over their lifetime.
  • 1.5 to 3 — optimize. Workable, but improve retention or pricing to push it higher.
  • 3 and above — scalable. Every ad dollar returns multiples; scaling acquisition is profitable.

How ProfitPulse computes it

LTV = gross margin per unit × (100 ÷ monthly churn %). Then LTV:CAC = LTV ÷ CAC. With the defaults (price $49, variable cost $10, CAC $30, 5% churn), gross margin is $39/unit, LTV is $780, and LTV:CAC is an impressive 26x.
