Target-Margin Pricing

Why unit economics matter (and how to use this calculator)

Before you raise a round, hire a team, or scale ads, you need to know three numbers cold: gross margin per unit, contribution after CAC, and LTV:CAC. Unit economics tell you whether each sale actually makes money after you pay to acquire it.

Gross margin per unit

Price − Variable cost. This is what each unit earns before marketing spend. Aim for a healthy rate (60–90% for software; lower for productized goods).

Contribution after CAC

(Price − Variable cost) − Acquisition cost. This is the true profit per customer and the number that decides whether scaling ads is profitable.

LTV : CAC

Lifetime value ÷ acquisition cost. Below 1.5 you're probably losing money; 3+ (as shown when you compute) is the gold standard for a scalable SaaS business.

Breakeven customers

Monthly fixed cost ÷ contribution per unit. How many paying customers you need to cover your fixed costs each month. ProfitPulse surfaces this instantly.
