Target-Margin Pricing

How to price a SaaS product

Pricing is the highest-leverage decision in a SaaS business. A small change in price lands straight on margin, but it also changes how many customers buy. The target-margin calculator above finds the price that hits a specific gross-margin goal based on your cost.

Target-margin pricing formula

Unit price = Unit cost ÷ (1 − target margin). For a $20 cost and a 70% margin, the price is $20 ÷ 0.30 = $66.67. The calculator also applies discount and tax, and reports the realized margin so you can see what you actually keep after a discount.

Value-based vs. cost-plus

Cost-plus pricing keeps you safe but can leave money on the table. Value-based pricing — anchoring on the outcome your product creates — often supports a higher price. Use the calculator to find your floor, then validate the ceiling with customer research.

Three pricing patterns that work for SaaS

  • Freemium — free tool acquires users, paid tier converts power users (ProfitPulse's model).
  • Tiered by usage — price scales with seats, volume or features.
  • Annual commitment — discount annual plans to boost cash flow and reduce churn.
